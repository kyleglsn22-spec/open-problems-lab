# Teschner's bondage-number conjecture

## Statement
The bondage number `b(G)` is the least number of edges whose deletion increases the domination number `γ(G)`.
Teschner (1995) conjectured `b(G) ≤ (3/2) Δ(G)` for every finite graph with at least one edge.

## Known results
| Bound or result | Reference | Checked by us? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| True when `γ(G) ≤ 3` | Teschner 1995 | No |
| False: a connected cubic bipartite graph on 18 vertices with `γ = 6`, `b = 5 > 9/2` | Yavari 2609.04257 | Yes (BOND-1) |

Statement notes for the Auditor:
- `IsDominating G D`: every vertex is in `D` or adjacent to a vertex of `D`. `dominationNumber`, `bondageNumber` are
  `sInf`s; for Yavari's graph both sets are shown nonempty.
- `bondageNumber G = sInf {k | ∃ F ⊆ E(G), |F| = k, γ(G) < γ(G − F)}`.
- Proof: `γ ≥ 6` by checking all sorted 5-tuples of vertices (`GPart*.lean`); deleting the 5 edges at vertices 0 and 6
  isolates both, so `γ ≥ 7` (Yavari's argument); for every multiset of 4 edge indices, one of 57 listed minimum
  dominating sets keeps a surviving edge in every "bundle" (Yavari's Lemma 2.2), checked in `QPart*.lean`.
- The heavy checks are split into 25 small files on one import chain (each ≤ 7.5 GB to build, ≤ 6.8 GB to replay).
