# Goddard–Henning Conjecture 32

## Statement
Every planar triangulation on at least four vertices has a proper 4-colouring with classes `C₁, …, C₄` such that
`C₁ ∪ C₂` and `C₃ ∪ C₄` are total dominating sets (every vertex has a neighbour in each). Equivalently, the vertex
set splits into two total dominating sets that both induce bipartite subgraphs.

## Known results
| Bound or result | Reference | Checked by us? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weaker form (two disjoint total dominating sets) holds for all planar triangulations | Francis–Illickan–Jose–Rajendraprasad 2024 | No |
| False: a 36-vertex triangulation `B` (three copies of a 14-vertex `G₁₄` glued around an edge); for every `n ≥ 36` | Kwon–Park–Sano 2611.00572, Thm 1.2 | Yes (GHT-1, the `n = 36` case) |
| True with at most two even-degree vertices; the Francis et al. 3-colours-seen conjecture holds for triangulations | Kwon–Park–Sano, Thms 1.3, 1.5 | No |

Statement notes for the Auditor:
- "Planar triangulation on `n ≥ 4` vertices" is formalized as: a graph on `Fin n` with a straight-line planar drawing
  (`StraightLinePlanar`, reused from claim AB-1; by Fáry's theorem equivalent to planar) and exactly `3n − 6` edges
  (planar graphs with `3n − 6` edges are exactly the maximal planar graphs, i.e. triangulations).
- The conclusion is the paper's equivalent form (a): `A`, `Aᶜ` total dominating, `induce A`, `induce Aᶜ` 2-colourable.
- `B`'s edge list is generated by `explorer/total-domination/build.py` from the paper's TikZ edge list of `G₁₄`
  (36 edges) and the gluing in Section 3; `audit/goddard_henning_check.py` rebuilds it and compares.
- Proof: (a) ⇒ partner colouring (`partnerColoring_of_partition`, Prop. 2.3); Lemma 3.1 on `G₁₄` is checked by the
  kernel in a nested form (`lemma31`); `no_partner_coloring_B` applies it to the three copies of `G₁₄` in `B` (their
  edges map to edges of `B`, and `v₃, v₅, v₉` keep their neighbourhoods, `nbr_i_j`), so none of `az₂, bz₂, ab` is a
  partner edge, impossible on the triangle `a b z₂`.
